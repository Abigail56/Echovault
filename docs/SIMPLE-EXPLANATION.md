# EchoVault Nexus, explained simply

**Think of it as a smart, private notebook for your work and studies.**

---

## The problem

We all learn useful things every day, but they end up scattered across chats, screenshots, random files and our memory. When we need them later, we can't find them.

We also make decisions ("let's do it this way") and later forget *why* we made them. So we waste time arguing about the same thing again.

---

## What EchoVault Nexus does

It gives you one place to keep everything, organised and connected:

1. **Notes (the Vault):** like pages in a notebook. You write down anything you learn.
2. **Projects:** like folders. You put related notes together, for example "My final-year project" or "Website launch".
3. **Decisions:** like a diary of choices. For each decision you write what the situation was, what options you had, what you chose and why. Later you add how it actually turned out.
4. **Search:** like a search bar for your own brain. Type a word and it finds every note, project or decision that mentions it, even if you only type part of the word.
5. **AI assistant:** like asking a helper who has read *only your notebook*. You ask "What did we decide about X, and why?" It answers using your own notes and shows you exactly which notes it used, so you can check it. If your notebook doesn't have the answer, it tells you honestly instead of making something up.

---

## A simple example

Ada is building a website.

- She writes a **note**: "How to stop people overloading our website."
- She creates a **project**: "Website launch", and puts the note in it.
- She records a **decision**: "We'll limit each user to 100 requests a minute, because it's fair and cheap."
- Two months later she forgets the details. She asks the assistant: *"What limit did we choose and why?"*
- It answers: *"100 per minute, because it's fair and cheap"*, and shows her the note and decision it came from.

---

## Is it safe?

- Only **you** can see your notes. Other users can't, even if they try.
- Your password is scrambled in a way that can't be reversed, so it's never stored as plain text.
- The AI only sees the few notes related to your question, and only when you ask.
- You can download all your data, or delete your account completely, at any time.

---

## In one sentence

> **EchoVault Nexus is a private digital notebook that remembers what you learned and why you made your decisions, and lets you find or ask about anything in seconds.**
